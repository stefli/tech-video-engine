# Asset provenance

The files in this folder are downscaled copies of images credited to **Tesla**
in TechCrunch's 2026-10-15 Roadster report:
https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/tesla-reveals-spacex-powered-roadster-that-flies-and-falls-with-style/

They are used here as editorial reference for a private rough cut. Tesla's
media/brand terms still apply. Do not publish or monetize this project without
permission or a confirmed editorial-use license.

No image is labeled as independent testing. Every moving/generated image in the
video is labeled `官方素材 / 概念示意` or `概念示意` on screen.
